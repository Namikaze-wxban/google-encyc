Google.com is the primary web domain used by Google for its search engine and a range of related internet services. The website is primarily known for allowing users to search for information available on the World Wide Web.

Google Search processes search queries and presents results that may include webpages, images, videos, news, maps, knowledge panels and other forms of information. The service is available in many languages and is used by people around the world.

History

Google originated from a research project developed by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were students at Stanford University. Their work focused on improving the organization and ranking of information on the World Wide Web.

Google was incorporated as a company in 1998. Its search technology subsequently became one of the most widely used methods for finding information on the internet.

Google Search

Google Search is the main service associated with Google.com. It uses automated systems to discover, organize and index information from websites and other sources.

When a user submits a query, Google's systems evaluate many signals to determine which information is most relevant to the query. Search results can change over time as webpages, information and ranking systems change.

Website and interface

The Google.com homepage is known for its relatively simple interface. A central search field allows users to enter queries, while additional features may provide access to other Google services.

Technology

Google's search infrastructure consists of large-scale computing systems designed to crawl, index and retrieve information. Automated programs commonly referred to as crawlers discover webpages and collect information that can be used by search systems.

Search infrastructure operates across distributed computing systems. Ranking and retrieval technologies are designed to process large numbers of queries and return relevant information efficiently.

Business model

Google operates a large internet advertising business. Advertising can appear alongside or within various Google products. The company also generates revenue from cloud services, subscriptions, hardware and other commercial activities.

Privacy and security

Google provides security mechanisms intended to protect its services and users. The company also provides privacy controls through various account and product settings.

Users should distinguish between Google's legitimate websites and unrelated websites that imitate Google's branding. A webpage using Google's name or visual appearance does not necessarily belong to Google.
